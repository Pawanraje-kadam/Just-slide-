How Does NAT Break End-to-End Connectivity? | Intermediate | Routing | Network address translation rewrites addresses and ports at a boundary, so hosts outside cannot initiate connections to inside peers without mappings or relays. | NAT explains why peer-to-peer systems need STUN, TURN, or hole punching. | Why can two clients behind different NATs sometimes connect directly and sometimes not? | nat, p2p, internet

What Does a Vector Database Actually Index? | Intermediate | Vector Search | Vector databases trade exhaustive comparison for approximate neighbor graphs or clusters, balancing recall against latency and memory. | Retrieval quality in RAG systems depends on index structure and search parameters that are rarely explained in tutorials. | How do recall and latency respond when you change the number of probes in an approximate index? | vector-search, ann, retrieval
Why Is Retrieval Quality Harder to Measure Than Answer Quality? | Intermediate | Vector Search | A retrieved document can be relevant without helping the answer, and an answer can sound right despite weak evidence. Both require separate judgments. | RAG teams routinely tune the wrong layer because they only evaluate the final text. | Which retrieval metrics would you track, and how would you label relevance at scale? | rag, retrieval-evaluation, metrics
How Do Engineers Decide Between Fine-Tuning and Retrieval? | Advanced | Model Serving | Fine-tuning changes behavior; retrieval changes available evidence. Choosing between them depends on whether the gap is style, domain knowledge, or missing context. | The wrong choice adds cost and complexity while failing to fix the actual failure mode. | Which failure cases would fine-tuning solve that retrieval cannot, and vice versa? | fine-tuning, rag, trade-offs
Why Does GPU Utilization Rarely Reach Its Theoretical Peak? | Advanced | Inference Systems | Memory stalls, small batch sizes, kernel overhead, and synchronization leave compute idle even on expensive accelerators. Headline throughput assumes ideal conditions. | Accelerator cost is dominated by utilization, so understanding idle time matters as much as choosing the best chip. | What workload and batching choices would keep an accelerator busy without blowing latency targets? | gpu, utilization, performance
How Should AI Systems Communicate Uncertainty to Users? | Intermediate | AI Observability | A probability is not a sentence a user can act on. What Makes an Agent's Tool Calls Reliable? | Intermediate | Agent Infrastructure | Agents fail at the boundaries: malformed arguments, stale state, ambiguous tools, and silent partial failures.
